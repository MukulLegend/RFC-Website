Our Journey

Founded by football enthusiast Mukul with Co-Founder Varun our club began its journey as 21-FC. United by a shared passion for the beautiful game, we aimed to cultivate a community where talent and teamwork thrive.

As our aspirations grew, so did our identity. Drawing inspiration from the legendary Spanish center-back, Sergio Ramos—renowned for his leadership, resilience, and dedication—we rebranded ourselves as Ramos Football Club. This transformation symbolizes our commitment to embodying the qualities that make champions.

Our Vision

At Ramos FC, we envision a nurturing environment where players develop not only their skills but also their character. Our focus is on:

  • Fostering teamwork and unity on and off the field.
  • Encouraging discipline, perseverance, and sportsmanship.
  • Providing structured training programs to hone individual talents.
  • Building a community that supports and celebrates collective growth.

What Sets Us Apart

Our club stands out through:

  • A player-centric approach that prioritizes personal and athletic development.
  • An inclusive culture welcoming individuals from diverse backgrounds.
  • A commitment to continuous improvement and embracing challenges.
  • Leadership that mentors and motivates, mirroring the ethos of our namesake.

Join Our Legacy

Whether you're an aspiring player, a dedicated coach, or a passionate supporter, Ramos Football Club invites you to be part of our evolving story. Together, we aim to make a lasting impact on the world of football.
