Our Legacy

Founded as 21-FC by Mukul, Varun, and Roshan, our club has evolved into Ramos Football Club, drawing inspiration from the legendary Spanish center-back, Sergio Ramos. Our journey reflects dedication, resilience, and a commitment to excellence.

Our Vision

  • Foster teamwork and unity on and off the field.
  • Encourage discipline, perseverance, and sportsmanship.
  • Provide structured training programs to hone individual talents.
  • Build a community that supports and celebrates collective growth.
